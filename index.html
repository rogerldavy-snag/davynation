About Me

Hello, I am Divine.

I grew up in East Flatbush, in the heart of Little Caribbean.

I started as a roadie in 1996, and by 1999 I was road manager for 50 Cent and others. The road taught me how to run a show, keep people moving, and handle pressure.

My degrees: a Bachelors degree in Communication from Northeastern University and a Masters degree in teaching students with disabilities from Brooklyn College.

I brought that same discipline into education, and into tax preparation, where I have worked since 2005. I write it all down now, in the Playbook and in a memoir that is still being written, so the lessons do not stay locked in one person.

Tax preparation and teaching sit at the same intersection for me. One is doing the work for a client. The other is showing a preparer how the work is done. Both need plain language, preparation, and follow through.

My credo is simple: God, Family, Music and Bikes. Everything else is a distraction.

Business, tech, and tax preparer news

Snapshot gathered on Sunday, November 1, 2026. Headlines link out to the source named under each one. This page does not update itself; a new day brings a new snapshot.

Business and tech

  • November 1, 2026 · TradingView, from JCN Newswire

    Hitachi plans a new global digital services company for April 2027

    Hitachi Digital will unite GlobalLogic and Hitachi Digital Services under one brand, with about 35,000 professionals in 27 countries and more than 880 active clients, focused on cloud, data, and AI.

  • October 30, 2026 · Unite.AI

    Google’s orbiting TPU sends back its first AI answer, a haiku

    Project Suncatcher ran the open Gemma 4 model on a data center class TPU in orbit and downlinked a haiku over Svalbard. Power limits each run to about 20 minutes, and two linked satellites are planned for 2027.

  • November 1, 2026 · Reuters

    Binance resumes withdrawals after covering most of $6.3 billion stolen

    Binance said it covered 95% of the tokens stolen in the largest digital heist on record, using company money to buy replacements. The FBI tied the attack to the North Korean unit known as TraderTraitor.

  • November 1, 2026 · Reuters

    South Korea exports nearly double in October, strongest growth since 1974

    Exports rose 93.7% from a year earlier to $115.28 billion, with a $42.50 billion trade surplus, a bellwether reading for global trade.

Tax preparer news and announcements

  • October 22, 2026 · JD Supra, Morgan Lewis

    IRS updates retirement plan corrections and creates a new IRA correction program

    Revenue Procedure 34 of 2026 expands the Employee Plans Compliance Resolution System, effective October 22. Revenue Procedure 33 of 2026 creates the IRA Compliance Resolution System, effective May 1, 2027, and 60 day rollover waiver requests move there from private letter rulings over 2027 and 2028.

  • October 22, 2026 · IRS

    IRS reminder: get an Identity Protection PIN before the mid November maintenance window

    Taxpayers filing early in 2027 should request an IP PIN before the online system goes offline for annual maintenance in mid November. It is generally back in mid January. The IRS will never ask for an IP PIN by call, email, text, or social media.

  • October 26, 2026 · AskFeather, summarizing IRS guidance of October 23

    PTIN renewal is $18.75 a year, and ID.me changes how preparers update their details

    The PTIN registration and renewal fee is $18.75 per year for calendar years 2026 and 2027. Preparers who link a PTIN account to ID.me must now change a name, date of birth, or email address through the ID.me account.

  • October 21, 2026 · TIGTA report, summarized by the Oklahoma Society of Enrolled Agents

    Watchdog: noncompliant PTINs behind millions of returns

    TIGTA found more than 38,000 return preparers with a noncompliant PTIN status prepared over 2 million returns in processing year 2025. The report is a reminder to check PTIN status before the season, not after it.
